Common Questions

Pricing Questions, Answered.

Do prices include domain registration and hosting?

No. Website package prices cover design, development, and deployment assistance. Domain registration and recurring hosting are separate costs billed at whatever your chosen provider charges. NTX will assist with setup and configuration.

Is the AI Intake monthly service really required?

Yes. The monthly service keeps the system hosted, monitored, maintained, and operational. Without it the system cannot run. If you cancel, the intake assistant is deactivated at the end of the paid billing period.

Does the Business + SEO package guarantee Google rankings?

No, and we will never make that promise. Business + SEO is an initial local-search setup — it creates stronger technical foundations and improves how search engines understand your business. Actual rankings depend on competition, location, and factors outside NTX's control.

Can I start with just a website and add automation later?

Yes. Many clients start with a website and add the AI Intake Assistant once the site is live. NTX builds websites with future additions in mind.

How many text messages are included?

The Standard plan includes up to 500 SMS segments per month, which covers typical volume for a single-location local business. Higher message volume may cost more — we'll quote it based on your actual usage.

What gets billed separately?

Twilio phone-number, carrier, and A2P registration fees, plus any third-party subscriptions, may be billed separately at cost. Additional locations, phone numbers, integrations, dashboards, and major workflow changes require a custom quote.

Why do AI plans say "starting at"?

Setup pricing depends on your industry, how many intake questions the assistant needs to handle, and what it connects to. The listed price is the starting point for a straightforward single-location setup — your exact price is confirmed before any work begins.

How does payment work?

Payment terms are discussed on your free call. Nothing starts until you have a clear scope and know exactly what you're paying for. There are no surprise fees.

What if I need something not listed here?

Request a custom quote through the contact form or book a call. NTX will put together the right scope for your business without unnecessary add-ons.
