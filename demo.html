About the System

What This Demo Is Showing You.

The NTX AI Intake Demo is an interactive demonstration of the automation system NTX builds and configures for local service businesses. What you experience in the demo is representative of what a business's customers experience when the system is live.

The system is designed to handle the gap between a missed call or unanswered inquiry and the moment a business owner can respond — keeping the lead engaged and collecting relevant information automatically.
