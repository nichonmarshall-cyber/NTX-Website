How It Works

The System Handles It While You Work

When a call comes in that you can't answer, the system takes over. It reaches out, collects the information you need, and keeps the conversation going — so the lead doesn't go cold before you have a chance to respond.

  • Missed Call Text-Back The moment you miss a call, the customer gets a text automatically. They know someone is aware of them — and they stay engaged instead of calling your competitor.
  • Collects Customer Info The system asks for their name, what they need, and any relevant details — like vehicle info for auto businesses. You get that information before you ever call them back.
  • Keeps the Conversation Moving It doesn't just send one message and stop. It keeps the exchange going at the right pace, so leads stay warm until they're ready to book.
  • Moves Them Toward Booking Once someone is ready, the system can direct them to your booking link or hand the conversation off to you — with all the context already in hand.
