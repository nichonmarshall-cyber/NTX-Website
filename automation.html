The Problem

Busy Businesses Lose Jobs They Never Knew About.

When you're working, you can't always pick up. Most customers won't wait — and you have no way of knowing what you missed.

01

Missed Calls

Most people won't call back or leave a voicemail. If no one picks up, they move on to the next business on the list.

02

Slow Replies

A lead texts at 6pm asking about pricing. By the time you see it the next morning, they've already booked someone else.

03

No Follow-Up

Someone asked for a quote and went quiet. Without a follow-up, that lead disappears — even if they still needed the job done.

How It Works

The System Handles It While You Work.

NTX builds and connects an automated system to your existing phone number and business. Once it's running, it responds to leads, collects their information, and keeps the conversation moving — without you having to do anything.

You get the lead information. The customer gets a response. Nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Missed Call Text-Back The moment you miss a call, the customer automatically gets a text. They stay engaged instead of moving on.
  • Automated Lead Intake The system asks for their name, what they need, and relevant details. You get that information before you call them back.
  • Keeps the Conversation Moving It doesn't send one message and stop. It keeps the exchange going at the right pace so leads stay warm.
  • Moves Toward Booking Once someone is ready, the system directs them to your booking link or hands the conversation off to you with full context.
  • 24/7 Availability Leads that come in at 8pm, on weekends, or during busy hours all get an immediate response — same as if you'd picked up.
